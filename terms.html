Demo

A new organisation may use Safety Premium free for one month. The demo ends automatically unless a subscription is arranged.

Subscription and payment

  • Prices are in Philippine peso (₱) and listed on the Subscription page.
  • Messerschmidts Safety Premium: ₱1,000 per user per month.
  • Invited subcontractor accounts: ₱100 per month when working only on the site(s) of the inviting account. A subcontractor account that opens a new site of its own pays the full ₱1,000 per month.
  • Messerschmidts EAM Standard: ₱20,000 per month per organisation, up to 25 users included, and ₱750 per month for each additional user. Larger organisations by quotation.
  • Messerschmidts Construction: ₱50,000 per month per organisation, for any number of sites, with a 30-day free trial. It includes Messerschmidts Safety for the organisation’s own users on its sites. Subcontractor accounts on the organisation's sites are included too.
  • An administrator subscribes or renews in Settings > Subscription in the app. The app sends reminders 7, 3 and 1 days before the demo or a paid month ends.
  • Payment is made in the app by e-wallet, card, QR Ph or online banking through PayMongo, or by USDT on TRON, and is verified automatically. [Lawyer: billing cycle, late payment, taxes, refunds.]
