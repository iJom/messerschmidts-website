Modules

Everything the safety office runs

Permits to work

Hot work, confined space, work at height, electrical isolation and more, with approvals, extensions and close-out.

Man-hours

Daily headcount and hours by company and site, with safe man-hours counted automatically.

Incidents

Report injuries, near misses and property damage, investigate, and track corrective actions to closure.

Competency & medical records

Trainings, certificates and fitness-to-work records, with reminders before anything expires.

ID cards with QR badges

Print ID cards for workers and visitors. A QR scan shows who the person is and whether they are cleared.

Gate & vehicle passes

Issue passes for people, vehicles and equipment, and record every entry and exit at the gate.

Security log

Guards record rounds, observations and events in a log the safety office can review.

Typhoon preparedness

Checklists and status by signal level, so every site knows what to secure and who is responsible.

Memos & announcements

Publish memos to sites and teams, and show urgent notices on a ticker tape across the app.

Toolbox talks

Plan and record daily talks with topic, attendees and signatures.

Posters & stickers

Print safety posters, inspection stickers and signage from ready templates.

Offline devices

Gates, safety officers and guards keep recording without a connection; data syncs later.
