HSSE

Health, Safety, Security and Environment in one view

Records are grouped by domain, so each specialist has a home and management sees the whole picture.

It helps you keep the records that DOLE occupational safety and health rules ask for under RA 11058 and DO 198-18: permits to work, toolbox talks, incident reports and man-hours.

Health

Medical clearances, fitness to work, first aid cases and health campaigns.

Safety

Permits, inspections, incidents, toolbox talks and man-hours.

Security

Gate and vehicle passes, ID badges, guard rounds and the security log.

Environmental

Spills, waste, housekeeping and environmental observations.
