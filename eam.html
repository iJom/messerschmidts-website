Messerschmidts EAM

Maintenance that is planned, not panicked

An enterprise asset management and CMMS application for plants and facilities. Work orders, preventive maintenance, assets, stores, procurement and budget, in one place that keeps working offline.

Talk to us about EAM See EAM plans
Modules

Everything the maintenance team touches

Work orders

Requests, approvals, assignment, labour, parts used and sign-off. Every job keeps its full history and photos.

Preventive maintenance

Schedules by calendar, run hours or meter readings. Work orders are created on time, every time.

Assets

A register of equipment by site, area and system, with nameplate data, documents, warranties and failure history.

Inventory & stores

Stock levels by store and bin, minimum and reorder points, issues against work orders, and stock counts.

Procurement

Purchase requests and orders linked to the parts and jobs that need them, with receiving into stores.

Budget

Plan maintenance spend by site and cost centre, and see actual labour and parts costs against it.

Incidents

Record equipment failures and near misses, link them to assets and follow up with corrective work orders.

Reports

Backlog, PM compliance, mean time between failures, downtime and spend. Printable and exportable.

Offline devices

Technicians close jobs, read meters and log parts on phones and tablets with no signal.

Signed updates

Software updates are signed. Your server checks the signature before it installs anything.

Work orders

One record from request to sign-off

Anyone can raise a request. Planners turn it into a work order, assign it, and see it through.

  • Priorities, due dates and failure codes
  • Labour hours and spare parts booked against the job
  • Checklists and photos captured on site
  • Supervisor sign-off before the job is closed
  • Links to permits to work when Messerschmidts Safety is in use
  • RequestedOperator
  • ApprovedPlanner
  • AssignedTechnician
  • In progress (offline)Field
  • CompletedTechnician
  • Signed offSupervisor

Illustration of a typical work order flow.

Preventive maintenance

Fewer breakdowns, fewer weekend call-outs

Set up maintenance plans once. The system creates the work orders when they are due, by date or by meter reading, and shows what slipped.

  • Weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly tasks
  • Run-hour and meter based triggers
  • Task lists with required parts reserved in stores
  • PM compliance per site and per team
Stores and procurement

The right part, on the shelf

Know what is in each store, what is reserved for upcoming jobs, and what needs ordering. Purchase requests come straight from low stock or from a work order.

  • Multiple stores and bins per site
  • Reorder points and suggested orders
  • Receiving against purchase orders
  • Costs flow into budget reports
Deployment

Runs where you need it

On your server

Keep your data inside your own network. The application runs on a server you control.

Or hosted by us

No server to look after. We run it and keep it up to date for you.

Offline and sync

Devices work without a connection and sync with whichever server you use.
