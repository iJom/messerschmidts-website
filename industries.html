Solar and power plants

Large sites, few people, poor signal. A 50 MW solar plant can have hundreds of inverters and strings to inspect.

The usual headaches

  • Remote areas with weak or no mobile data
  • Many identical assets to keep track of
  • Energised work that needs strict permits
  • Typhoon season planning
With EAM
  • Asset register down to inverter, combiner box and tracker
  • PM schedules by calendar or run hours
  • Spare parts for critical items always in stock
  • Downtime and failure reports
With Safety
  • Electrical isolation and hot work permits
  • Contractor badges and gate control
  • Typhoon preparedness by signal level
  • Man-hours for every contractor

Construction

Hundreds of workers from many subcontractors, changing every week. The site office needs to know who is on site and whether they are cleared to work.

The usual headaches

  • Many subcontractors with their own crews
  • Inductions, trainings and medical clearances
  • Gate control for people and vehicles
  • Monthly man-hour and incident reports for the client
With EAM
  • Equipment and plant hire register
  • Maintenance of generators, cranes and vehicles
  • Stores for consumables and PPE
With Safety
  • ID cards with QR badges for every worker
  • Subcontractor accounts at ₱50 on the same site
  • Permits, toolbox talks and incidents
  • Posters, stickers and announcements

Facilities

Buildings, campuses and portfolios with many systems and many occupants making requests.

The usual headaches

  • Requests arrive by phone, chat and e-mail
  • Statutory inspections for lifts, fire and electrical systems
  • Contractors on site every day
With EAM
  • One place for requests and work orders
  • PM for HVAC, lifts, pumps and fire systems
  • Budget per building and cost centre
With Safety
  • Permits for contractors working in occupied areas
  • Visitor and vehicle passes
  • Security rounds and log

Manufacturing

Every hour a line stands still costs money. Maintenance and safety have to work as one team.

The usual headaches

  • Unplanned breakdowns on critical lines
  • Spare parts scattered across stores
  • Lock-out and tag-out on running equipment
With EAM
  • Breakdown and PM work orders by line
  • Stores, reorder points and purchasing
  • MTBF, downtime and spend reports
With Safety
  • Lock-out permits linked to work orders
  • Daily toolbox talks
  • Incident and near-miss reporting
