Maintenance for Philippine power plants

Messerschmidts EAM is built around the assets of solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, biomass and diesel or bunker plants, substations and transmission lines. Messerschmidts Safety covers the people who do the work, and Messerschmidts Construction runs construction projects. The same apps also run facilities and factories. Jump to your site:

Solar plants

Large sites, few people, poor signal. A 50 MW solar plant in Luzon or Mindanao can have hundreds of inverters and thousands of strings to inspect.

The usual headaches

  • Many identical assets to keep track of
  • Remote areas with weak or no mobile data
  • Breakdowns that cost generation
  • Typhoon season planning
  • Energised work and outside contractors
With EAM
  • Asset register down to inverter, combiner box and tracker
  • PM schedules by calendar or run hours
  • Spare parts for critical items always in stock
  • Downtime and failure reports
With Safety
  • Electrical isolation and hot work permits
  • Contractor badges and gate control
  • Typhoon preparedness by signal level
  • Man-hours for every contractor

Wind, hydro, geothermal, biomass and diesel plants

Rotating plant, high pressure and heavy fuel. Turbines, generators, boilers and engines need maintenance by run hours, and every shutdown brings contractors and permits.

The usual headaches

  • Overhauls planned by run hours
  • Critical spares far from the supplier
  • Island and off-grid plants far from support
  • Confined space and work at height
  • Many contractors on site during shutdowns
With EAM
  • PM by run hours or meter for turbines, generators, pumps and engines
  • Spare parts for bearings, seals, filters and injectors
  • Overhaul work orders with labour and parts
  • Downtime and failure history per unit
With Safety
  • Confined space, work at height and hot work permits
  • Lock-out permits linked to work orders
  • Toolbox talks with attendance
  • Spill, waste and housekeeping observations

Substations and transmission

Switchyards, lines and towers spread over many kilometres, with crews moving between sites every day.

The usual headaches

  • Assets spread over many kilometres
  • Short outage windows for maintenance
  • Crews moving between sites
  • Energised equipment and work at height
With EAM
  • Register of transformers, breakers, relays and towers
  • Inspection rounds and PM per substation and line
  • Spare parts for critical items
  • Full history per asset
With Safety
  • Electrical isolation permits
  • Work at height permits
  • Gate and vehicle passes for crews
  • Man-hours per contractor

Your records in one place

The apps keep the records in one place, in Manila time, ready to print or export. Your safety officer and compliance team still prepare and file the reports.

Maintenance records

Asset history, work orders and preventive maintenance per plant, from Messerschmidts EAM.

DOLE occupational safety and health

Helps you keep the records for RA 11058 and DO 198-18: permits to work, toolbox talks, incident reports and man-hours.

Your other reports

Keeps your maintenance and safety records in one place for your reports.

Other industries

Construction

Hundreds of workers from many subcontractors, changing every week. The site office needs to know who is on site and whether they are cleared to work.

Messerschmidts Construction is live. Schedule, inspections, deliveries and payments in its own app, with Safety included for your own users. EAM looks after the equipment. Explore Construction or ask for a demo.

The usual headaches

  • Many subcontractors with their own crews
  • Inductions, trainings and medical clearances
  • Gate control for people and vehicles
  • Monthly man-hour and incident reports for the client
With Construction
  • Executive dashboard, progress S-curve, SPI and CPI
  • WBS, Gantt and lookaheads
  • WIR, MIR and T&C inspections
  • Deliveries and requests for payment

Explore Construction

With EAM
  • Equipment and plant hire register
  • Maintenance of generators, cranes and vehicles
  • Stores for consumables and PPE
With Safety
  • ID cards with QR badges for every worker
  • Subcontractor accounts at ₱100 on your site(s)
  • Permits, toolbox talks and incidents
  • Posters, stickers and announcements

Facilities

Buildings, campuses and portfolios with many systems and many occupants making requests.

The usual headaches

  • Requests arrive by phone, chat and e-mail
  • Statutory inspections for lifts, fire and electrical systems
  • Contractors on site every day
With EAM
  • One place for requests and work orders
  • PM for HVAC, lifts, pumps and fire systems
  • Budget per building and cost centre
With Safety
  • Permits for contractors working in occupied areas
  • Visitor and vehicle passes
  • Security rounds and log

Manufacturing

Every hour a line stands still costs money. Maintenance and safety have to work as one team.

The usual headaches

  • Unplanned breakdowns on critical lines
  • Spare parts scattered across stores
  • Lock-out and tag-out on running equipment
With EAM
  • Breakdown and PM work orders by line
  • Stores, reorder points and purchasing
  • MTBF, downtime and spend reports
With Safety
  • Lock-out permits linked to work orders
  • Daily toolbox talks
  • Incident and near-miss reporting
