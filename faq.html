Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Write to customerservice@messerschmidts.online.

Products

Is it made for power plants in the Philippines?

Yes. Messerschmidts EAM is built for the maintenance of solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, biomass and diesel or bunker plants, substations and transmission lines in the Philippines, and Messerschmidts Safety is its companion app. Prices are in pesos, you pay by GCash, Maya, card, online banking or crypto, and our customer service team is Filipino. It is also used on construction sites, in facilities and in factories.

Is there an app for construction?

Messerschmidts Construction is coming soon. Today, construction teams use Messerschmidts Safety for permits, toolbox talks, man-hours, gates and subcontractors, and Messerschmidts EAM for their equipment. Ask to be notified.

Does it help with DOLE records?

It helps you keep the records for them. Messerschmidts Safety keeps the permits, toolbox talks, incident reports and man-hours that DOLE occupational safety and health rules (RA 11058, DO 198-18) ask for. For your other reports, it keeps your maintenance and safety records in one place. Your safety officer and compliance team still prepare and file the reports.

What is the difference between EAM and Safety?

Messerschmidts EAM is for maintenance: work orders, preventive maintenance, assets, stores, procurement and budget. Messerschmidts Safety is for HSSE: permits, man-hours, incidents, people records, gates, security and announcements. They run in the same application and share sites and users.

Do I need both products?

No. You can use either one on its own and add the other later.

Which devices does it run on?

It runs in a modern web browser on computers, tablets and phones. Field devices can work offline.

Can we print ID cards, posters and stickers?

Yes. Messerschmidts Safety prints ID cards with QR badges, posters and inspection stickers from templates.

Offline, hosting and security

How does offline work?

Devices keep the data they need and record new entries locally. When a connection is available, the device syncs with the server. Conflicts are flagged for review rather than silently overwritten.

Can we host it ourselves?

Yes. The application can run on your own server inside your network, or on our hosted service.

What are signed updates?

Each software update is digitally signed. Your server checks the signature before installing it, so it only runs software that really comes from us.

Where is our data stored?

On the server you choose: your own, or ours. See the privacy page for details, which is still a draft.

Subscription and payment

How much does it cost?

Messerschmidts EAM Standard is ₱20,000 per month for the whole organisation, with up to 25 users included and ₱750 per month for each additional user. Bigger organisations can ask us for a quote. Messerschmidts Safety Premium is ₱1,000 per user per month. See the Subscription page.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. A new organisation gets one month of Safety Premium free as a demo.

How are subcontractors charged?

An invited subcontractor account costs ₱100 per month when it works only on the site(s) of the account that invited it. A subcontractor account that opens a new site of its own, for example an EPC or main contractor registered as a subcontractor, pays the full ₱1,000.

How do we pay?

By GCash, Maya, card, QR Ph or online banking through PayMongo, or by USDT on TRON (TRC-20). An administrator pays in Settings > Subscription in the app. The system verifies the payment automatically (USDT on TRON is detected automatically) and the plan activates instantly, with no manual confirmation.

How do we subscribe or renew?

An administrator opens Settings > Subscription in the app to subscribe or renew. The app reminds administrators by e-mail and with a banner 7, 3 and 1 days before the free month or the paid month ends.

Can we cancel?

Yes. Tell customer service before your next billing month. Ask us about exporting your data.
