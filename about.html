About

Software for the people who keep sites running

Messerschmidts makes two products that work as one: an EAM application for maintenance teams and an HSSE application for safety teams. We build them for real sites, with real connection problems.

What we believe

Maintenance and safety are two sides of the same job. The technician who repairs a pump needs a permit; the safety officer who approves it needs to know which equipment is involved. Keeping those records in separate systems, or on paper, costs time and creates gaps.

So we built one application with two products. Each can be used on its own, and both share the same sites, people and devices.

Offline first

If it does not work in the field, it does not work. Every key screen runs without a connection.

Your data, your choice

Run it on your own server or on ours. You can move between the two.

Plain pricing

Prices in Philippine peso, per user per month, paid by bank transfer or e-wallet.

Careful updates
