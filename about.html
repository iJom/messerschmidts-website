About

Software for the people who keep sites running

Messerschmidts makes three Philippine apps that work as one: an EAM application for maintenance teams, an HSSE application for safety teams and a Construction application for project teams. We build them for power plants and project sites in the Philippines, with real connection problems.

What we believe

MaintenanceSafety

Maintenance and safety are two sides of the same job. The technician who repairs a pump needs a permit; the safety officer who approves it needs to know which equipment is involved. Keeping those records in separate systems, or on paper, costs time and creates gaps.

So we built one platform. EAM and Safety share the same sites, people and devices, and Messerschmidts Construction, live since release 4.13.0, runs beside them on the same server, sign-in and data, with Safety included for your own users. Explore Construction.

Offline first

If it does not work in the field, it does not work. Every key screen runs without a connection.

Your data, your choice

Run it on your own server or on ours. You can move between the two.

Plain pricing
