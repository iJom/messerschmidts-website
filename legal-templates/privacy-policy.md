## 8. Your rights as a data subject (RA 10173, Sec. 16 to 18)

You have the right to:

1. **Be informed** that your personal information is being collected and processed, and why.
2. **Object** to processing, including for direct marketing or profiling, and to withdraw consent at any time.
3. **Access** your personal information, its sources, recipients, the reason for processing and how long we keep it.
4. **Rectification**: correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
5. **Erasure or blocking**: have your information suspended, withdrawn, removed or destroyed when it is incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, used for an unauthorised purpose, no longer necessary, or when you withdraw consent or object and there is no other lawful basis to keep it.
6. **Data portability**: get a copy of the information you gave us in an electronic, commonly used format.
7. **Damages**: be compensated for damage caused by inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of your information.
8. **File a complaint** with the National Privacy Commission.

These rights may be exercised by your lawful heirs or assigns after your death or incapacity (DPA Sec. 17).
