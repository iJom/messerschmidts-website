What we do

Three ways we help Philippine industries

Start with the one you need today. EAM and Safety share the same sites, people and sign-in.

01 · Messerschmidts EAM

EAM for Power Plants

Maintenance management for solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, biomass and diesel plants and substations.

  • Asset register with history and documents
  • Preventive maintenance by calendar, run hours or meter
  • Work orders from request to sign-off
  • Spare parts, stores and procurement
  • Downtime and failure reports
Explore EAM
02 · Messerschmidts Safety

HSSE

Health, safety, security and environment for the people on your site.

  • Permits to work and toolbox talks
  • Incidents and man-hours
  • Gate and vehicle passes
  • ID cards with QR badges
  • Helps you keep the records for DOLE safety rules (RA 11058, DO 198-18)
Explore Safety

Why Messerschmidts

Made for Philippine sites, not just offices

Solar farms far from town, hydro plants up in the mountains, island diesel plants, project sites with no fibre yet. The software has to keep up with where the work actually happens.

Philippine-made

Built in the Philippines for Philippine sites, in the plain English site teams use. Dates and reports in Manila time.

Works offline at remote sites

Phones and tablets keep recording with weak or no signal, and sync with the server when the connection comes back.

Prices in pesos

Clear monthly prices in Philippine peso. No foreign currency, no surprises on the exchange rate.

GCash and bank payment

Filipino customer service

Your server or ours
