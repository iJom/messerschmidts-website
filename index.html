What we do

Three ways we help Philippine industries

Start with the one you need today. All three run on the same server, sign-in and data.

01 · Messerschmidts EAMLive

EAM for Power Plants

Maintenance management for solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, biomass and diesel plants and substations.

  • Asset register with history and documents
  • Preventive maintenance by calendar, run hours or meter
  • Work orders from request to sign-off
  • Spare parts, stores and procurement
  • Downtime and failure reports
Explore EAM
02 · Messerschmidts SafetyLive

HSSE

Health, safety, security and environment for the people on your site.

  • Permits to work and toolbox talks
  • Incidents and man-hours
  • Gate and vehicle passes
  • ID cards with QR badges
  • Helps you keep the records for DOLE safety rules (RA 11058, DO 198-18)
Explore Safety
03 · Messerschmidts ConstructionLive

Construction

Project control for construction: schedule, inspections, deliveries and payments, with Safety included.

  • Executive dashboard with S-curve, SPI and CPI
  • WBS, Gantt and 2- and 6-week lookaheads
  • WIR, MIR and T&C inspections
  • Deliveries, purchase requests and requests for payment
  • Standards compliance matrix
