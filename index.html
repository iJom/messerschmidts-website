Field devices keep recording work orders, inspections and gate entries without a connection. Nothing is lost when the signal drops.

Syncs when it can

As soon as a device is back online, its records sync with the server and everyone sees the same picture.

FAQ

Common questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. Devices keep a local copy of what they need and record new entries offline. When the connection returns, the records sync with the server.

Can we run it on our own server?

Yes. You can run the application on a server you control, or use our hosted service. Updates are signed so your server can check they come from us.

How do we pay?

Customer service sends you the details for a bank transfer or e-wallet payment. We do not take card payments online.
