5. Points specific to our products

Safety

Messerschmidts Safety supports, but does not replace, qualified safety officers, safety committees, competent supervisors and the employer’s own safety and health program. The employer and its people remain responsible for meeting the Occupational Safety and Health Standards Act (RA 11058), its Implementing Rules (DOLE Department Order No. 198, Series of 2018), the DOLE Occupational Safety and Health Standards and other rules, including permits to work, lockout and isolation, inspections, training and reports to DOLE. Permit statuses, warnings, competency and medical checks, stop-work tools and checklists help people do this; they do not do it for them.

Weather, forecasts and Day Ahead Nominations

Weather forecasts and history in the apps come from MET Norway (Locationforecast) and NASA POWER. Forecasts are estimates and can be wrong, late or unavailable; NASA POWER data runs about two days behind and is research-grade. Generation forecasts, Day Ahead Nominations and other figures derived from weather data come with no guarantee. Always check the official sources, such as PAGASA advisories and warnings, and the instructions, schedules and deadlines of the grid and market operators (NGCP and IEMOP) before you nominate, dispatch, stop work or make any other decision.

Regulatory reports

The apps help prepare reports and forms for agencies such as the ERC, the DOE, NGCP and IEMOP, and for DOLE and DENR. The layouts are our own. You remain responsible for the content, accuracy, completeness and timeliness of everything you submit, for using the agency’s current form and channel, and for any penalty for a late, wrong or missing submission. Check every report before you sign or submit it.

Permits and requirements lists

Lists of permits, licences, clearances and requirements (for example for a construction project or a plant) are starting lists to help you plan. They are not legal advice and may be incomplete for your project, location or date. Requirements differ between local government units and change over time: always confirm them with the LGU (such as the building official and the business permits office) and the national agencies concerned, and get professional advice where needed.

Calculations

Figures the apps calculate, including earned value (EVM), S-curves and progress, schedules and lookaheads, billing and requests for payment, retention, taxes and withholding, budgets, man-hours, KPIs, stock and reorder levels, depend on the data entered and on the settings chosen, and may contain errors. They are not accounting, tax or engineering advice. Check them before relying on them, paying, invoicing, filing or reporting them.
