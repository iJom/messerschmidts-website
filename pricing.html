      Paying

      How payment works

      Pay the way you already pay in the Philippines. We do not take card payments online.

      • E-wallets: GCash, Maya
      • Banks: MariBank, UnionBank (InstaPay or PESONet)
      • Crypto: USDT on TRON (TRC-20), OKX Pay
      1. Request an account and use the free demo month.
      2. The app reminds administrators by e-mail and with a banner 7, 3 and 1 days before the free month or paid month ends.
      3. An administrator opens Settings > Subscription to subscribe or renew. It shows this month's amount in ₱ and our account numbers and QR codes.
      4. Pay, then press I have paid with the reference number or transaction ID.
      5. Customer service confirms it and your subscription continues without interruption.

      Questions about a payment? Our Filipino customer service team will help: customerservice@messerschmidts.online

      What happens after the free month?

      The app reminds administrators by e-mail and with a banner 7, 3 and 1 days before the free month ends. An administrator subscribes from Settings > Subscription; your data stays as it is either way.

      How do we renew each month?

      The same way: an administrator opens Settings > Subscription, pays and presses I have paid. Reminders come by e-mail and as a banner 7, 3 and 1 days before the paid month ends.

      Can I change the number of users?

      Yes. Tell customer service and the change applies from the next billing month.

      Do you charge VAT?

      Customer service will confirm tax on your quotation and invoice.

      Is hosting on our own server priced differently?

      Talk to us. Running on your own server may involve a set-up and support arrangement agreed with you.
