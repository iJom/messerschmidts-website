    Paying

    How payment works

    Pay the way you already pay in the Philippines. We do not take card payments online.

    • E-wallets: GCash, Maya
    • Banks: MariBank, UnionBank (InstaPay or PESONet)
    • Crypto: USDT on TRON (TRC-20), OKX Pay
    1. Request an account and use the free demo month.
    2. Open Settings, Subscription in the app: it shows this month's amount in ₱ and our account numbers and QR codes.
    3. Pay, then press I have paid with the reference number or transaction ID.
    4. Customer service confirms it and your subscription continues without interruption.

    customerservice@messerschmidts.online

    What happens after the free month?

    Customer service contacts you before the month ends. Subscribe from Settings, Subscription in the app; your data stays as it is either way.

    Can I change the number of users?

    Yes. Tell customer service and the change applies from the next billing month.

    Do you charge VAT?

    Customer service will confirm tax on your quotation and invoice.

    Is hosting on our own server priced differently?

    Talk to us. Running on your own server may involve a set-up and support arrangement agreed with you.
