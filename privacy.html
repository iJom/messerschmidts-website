Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · Effective and last updated: 25 Oct 2026

This policy explains what personal information Messerschmidts collects through this website and through Messerschmidts EAM, Messerschmidts Safety and Messerschmidts Construction (together, “the apps”), why we collect it, who we share it with, how we protect it, and what you can do about it. It follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC) first. For visitors and users outside the Philippines it also covers the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

See also our Website terms of use and Disclaimer. Using the apps is governed by the License Agreement and End-User Terms that each user accepts in the app.

2. Our two roles

  • Personal information controller. We decide how to use the information about visitors to this website, about people who ask for an account, buy or pay for a subscription or contact us, and the sign-in, security and acceptance records of app users.
  • Personal information processor. The records a customer organisation keeps in the apps (its sites, assets, permits, workers, visitors, incidents and so on) belong to that organisation, which is their controller. We process them only on its instructions, as set out in Schedule B (Data Processing Terms) of the License Agreement. If you are a worker or visitor whose details a customer entered, the customer is your first point of contact; we will pass your request to it and help it answer.

4. How we collect it

  • From you: when you request an account or start a demo (sign-up form), accept the End-User Terms, fill in your profile, upload files, send messages, subscribe or pay, or write to us. The contact form on this website does not send anything to us: it opens your own e-mail app with the message filled in.
  • From your organisation: administrators invite users and enter records about their workers, subcontractors and visitors.
  • From your use of the apps: sign-in, security and audit logs are made automatically.
  • From PayMongo: whether a payment succeeded, its amount, method and reference.

This website sets no cookies, uses no local storage, and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. It loads no third-party scripts or fonts; everything it uses is served from messerschmidts.online. The Subscription page asks app.messerschmidts.online for the public price list without cookies or personal data; like every web server, that server and the website host (GitHub Pages) see your IP address and browser type when you visit.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • Your organisation’s authorised users, according to the roles your administrator sets.
  • Service providers (processors) bound by confidentiality and data protection terms:
    • PayMongo, which processes card, e-wallet, QR Ph and online banking payments;
    • the e-mail (SMTP) service that sends the platform’s e-mails, such as invitations, reminders, receipts and reports;
    • hosting: the apps and their data run on our own (the operator’s) servers for hosted sites at app.messerschmidts.online, with network and security providers such as Cloudflare where used; this website is hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Weather services: MET Norway and NASA POWER. Our server sends them only the coordinates of a site to get forecasts and weather history. No personal information is sent to them.
  • Map tiles from OpenStreetMap: when a device shows a map online, it requests map tiles directly, which shows the provider the map area and the device’s IP address.
  • The optional AI mode, only if a user enters their own API key: the questions they ask go to that AI provider under its terms.
  • Government authorities and courts, such as the NPC, the BIR, DOLE or a court, when the law or a lawful order requires it.
  • A buyer or successor of the business, under the same protections, after notice to you.

7. Where it is stored, and international transfers

Hosted data is kept on the operator’s servers in the Philippines, unless an Order Form states another region. A customer that runs the apps on its own server keeps its data there. Offline devices keep a local copy until it syncs. Some service providers (for example the website host and network providers) may process limited data, such as IP addresses, outside the Philippines. We remain responsible for that data under RA 10173 Section 21, and we use contracts that require the same level of protection. For data from the EU/EEA or the UK, we rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or another safeguard the GDPR allows; ask us for a copy.

8. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all traffic between your devices and our servers.
  • Access control: role-based access (RBAC) per organisation, site and module, so users see only what their role allows; optional two-step sign-in with an authenticator app, which a server owner can make mandatory for administrators.
  • Audit logs of changes and sign-ins, and acceptance records that are hash-chained and digitally signed so that any change can be detected.
  • Regular backups of hosted data, kept for a limited period.
  • Only authorised personnel, bound by confidentiality, can reach the servers.

Personal data breaches. If a breach is likely to put people at real risk of serious harm, we notify the NPC and the affected people within 72 hours of knowing about it, as NPC Circular 16-03 requires, and, for people in the EU/EEA, the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours. Where we are the processor, we tell the customer without undue delay, and where possible within 24 hours, so it can notify on time.

9. See, change or delete your data

  • In the app: see and correct your profile under My account; your administrator can correct or remove records and users; administrators can export the organisation’s data files at any time.
  • By e-mail: write to customerservice@messerschmidts.online with “Data privacy” in the subject. Say what you want (a copy, a correction, deletion, an objection) and which account or organisation it concerns.
  • Response time: we confirm receipt within 5 working days and answer within 30 days. If a request is complex we may extend this as far as the law allows, and we tell you why within the first 30 days. Requests are free unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive.
  • We check your identity before acting, usually by replying to the e-mail address on your account. If someone acts for you, we ask for proof of their authority.
  • If your request is about records a customer organisation keeps, we forward it to that organisation and help it answer.

10. How long we keep it

  • Account and app data: while the subscription runs. After it ends, the organisation has a 30-day read-only window to export its data; we then delete it within 60 days, unless the law requires us to keep it. A deletion certificate is available on request.
  • Acceptance records: for the term of the License Agreement plus ten (10) years, or longer while a dispute is pending.
  • Billing and tax records: as long as the Philippine tax laws require.
  • Security and audit logs: sign-in logs (sign-ins, sessions, devices) are kept for 1 year; the audit trail is kept while the account is open and for 5 years after it closes, then deleted.
  • Backups: overwritten on a rolling basis after a limited period.
  • E-mails with us: as long as needed to deal with your question, then deleted or archived as business records.
  • Marketing consent: until you withdraw it.

11. Children

This website and the apps are business tools and are not directed at children. App users must be at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it. A customer that keeps records of workers under 18 in the apps is responsible for the lawful basis and consents for them.

12. Philippines: your rights under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

As a data subject you have the right to be informed; to object; to access your personal information; to have it corrected (rectification); to have it erased or blocked; to data portability (a copy in a commonly used electronic format); to be indemnified for damages caused by inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of your data; and to file a complaint. Contact our Data Protection Officer first (section 1). If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). Our lawful criteria are listed in section 5.
